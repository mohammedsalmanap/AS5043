## About

I work at the intersection of machine learning and full-stack development — building systems that run in production, not just prototypes that perform well on paper.

My paper **"AI-Driven Prediction of Building Energy and Comfort"** was presented at IEEE ICICACS-2026, won the **Best Paper Award**, and is now published on IEEE Xplore. The work uses Random Forest and LightGBM models to predict building energy consumption and thermal comfort across five Indian cities, achieving R² scores up to 0.9998.

I'm drawn to AI/ML and LLM-focused engineering, and enjoy taking an idea from a rough prototype to something that works reliably in the real world.
